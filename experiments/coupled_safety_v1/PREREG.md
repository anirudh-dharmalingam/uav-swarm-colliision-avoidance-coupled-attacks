# PREREG — Physical safety limits under coupled attacks (campaign coupled_safety_v1)

Frozen with `FROZEN_CODE.json` (sha256 of every runtime/analysis file, scenario table, specs, bounds) **before any confirmatory seed (13000+) was flown**. Development seeds 12000–12007 and calibration seeds 11000–11011 (train), 11100–11111 (holdout 1, consumed, see DEVIATIONS D1), 11200–11211 (holdout 2) were flown earlier and are not confirmatory.

## 1. Question and claims
When do a compromised UAV's physical motion and falsified self-reports exhaust the honest swarm's opportunity to preserve separation? We separate (i) physical impossibility (proved only in the 1-D restricted model, finite window), (ii) information limitation, (iii) conservative-controller rejection. No claim of a superior defense, of a guaranteed controller, of a sensing/inspection task, of a hardware result, or of RA-L acceptance is made or planned. Not claimed whatever the data show: any statement outside the restricted 1-D model about *impossibility* in 2-D/X500.

## 2. Mission, measurement and frozen constants
* Six X500 (PX4 v1.17 SITL, Gazebo Harmonic, world `worlds/coupled_safety/yard_physics.sdf`, sha256 in FROZEN_CODE.json): take off to 5 m, triangular lattice spacing 6 m, 20 Hz command loop, truth ≥ 50 Hz, transit 24 m at 1 m/s with scale 0.8 over the middle third (smoothstep ramps of 1/12 of the route at each end of the middle third), hold 5 s at the goal, land; 100 s allowed from the GCS start marker. Route heading, attacker id, onset, spawn jitter and network key are drawn **in advance** per scenario (`SCENARIOS.jsonl`; heading U[−20°,20°], onset U[8,12] s, jitter ≤ 0.25 m).
* Task scored = formation transit only. Route completion = ordered along-route progress (running max of honest-centroid projection on the route axis) ≥ 23 m (1 m tolerance) **and** no crash; nearest-polyline projection is not used.
* Separation thresholds: operational 1.5 m (not "collision"), proximity 0.8 m (≈ 2 × X500 rotor-tip envelope 0.39 m), crash = tilt > 60°. Gazebo contact events are **not available** in this harness (reported as such).
* Encounter window W = [onset, onset + 20 s] (fixed length). Primary per-episode endpoint **min_sep_ha** = min over W and honest j of the 3-D centre distance to the designated UAV (exists in every cell, including A0). Harm L = 1.5 m − min_sep_ha.
* Attack cells (labels): A0 truthful nominal · PHYS (= A8: ε = 2.5 m/s² toward the nearest received neighbour inside trigger radius 9 m, truthful report) · REPORT (= A4: position bias 1.25 m and velocity bias 0.5 m/s hiding closing, nominal motion) · COUPLED (= A14: identical PHYS and REPORT components). The designated UAV runs the fixed independent policy D0R in **all** cells, refuses refresh requests, never inherits the defender's policy; physical saturation is not hidden (requested = ‖u−u_nom‖ and realised acceleration are logged).
* Methods (labels → flags): D0R no filter · D24R interval filter, fixed geometry · D26 same rows/solver/fallback, supervisor (relaxation/link refresh/energy) differs · D24R_NOSQ interval filter with honest UAVs declining the squeeze (geometric-conservatism baseline, not an equal-deformation controller). All use `envelope = sound_age` (Gate A) and identical bounds.
* **Frozen bounds** (`bounds_declared.json`): U 3.0, slew 0.4 m/s² per 50 ms, h 0.4 s, M 8, d_safe 1.5, β_p 1.25 m, β_v 0.5 m/s, ego error 0.4 m / 1.1 m/s, sender error 0.4 m / 1.1 m/s, W 2.95 m/s², A_honest 6.2, A_any 8.8 m/s², T_net_max 0.15 s (= ceil₀.₀₅(1.15 × 0.123 s, max end-to-end transit over all 172 development episodes, incl. ROS/DDS), adverse regime 0.27 s = 0.12 s emulated + 0.15 s middleware). Derived from 24 HF1 flights (train 11000–11011; original-rule holdout 11100–11111 failed, revised rule validated on fresh holdout 11200–11211: **0 exceedances / 322,242 ticks on all five bounds**); see AUDIT.md and `calibration_bounds_pooled_rule.json`. HF1 has no network layer; transit bounds come from mission flights (dev).
* Setting selection (rule: first of (6 m,1 m/s), (8 m,1 m/s), (8 m,0.75 m/s) with all 8 benign episodes violation-free, valid-premise certified coverage ≥ 80 %, no harness fault): **none passed** (coverage 0.0 %, 33.0 %, 33.5 %). Hence the intended certified regime is **not established** and the study is a controlled vulnerability study at S0 = (6 m, 1 m/s) with explicit UNCERTIFIED labels. No guaranteed controller is promised.

## 3. Primary family (E1: 4 attacks × 4 methods × 30 paired scenarios, seeds 13000–13029, nominal network = 480 episodes)
Unit = scenario (complete paired blocks); 10,000 bootstrap resamples of scenarios (seed 20261025); two-sided percentile 95 % CI; two-sided bootstrap p (resolution 1e-4); **Holm** across three tests, all under D24R:
* **H1** interaction I = L_COUPLED − L_PHYS − L_REPORT + L_A0 (null I = 0; direction and size reported; I > 0 = super-additive harm).
* **H2** COUPLED − PHYS in min_sep_ha. **H3** COUPLED − REPORT in min_sep_ha.
Development expectation (8 dev scenarios, not confirmatory): I ≈ +0.84 m (sd 0.11), COUPLED−PHYS ≈ −0.96 m, COUPLED−REPORT ≈ −1.96 m. **Precision**: expected 95 % CI half-width for I at n = 30 ≈ 1.96·0.114/√30 = 0.041 m, minimum detectable effect (80 % power, α = 0.05) ≈ 0.058 m ≪ 1.5 m threshold → n = 30 retained (no extension). Binary outcomes (violation < 1.5 m, < 0.8 m, crash, safe task, route completion) are **secondary**, paired risk differences with Newcombe intervals; in development no episode violated 1.5 m, so floor effects are expected and an observed 0/n is not a zero-risk statement. D26/NOSQ/D0R comparisons are secondary performance trade-offs (no superiority claim). Everything not listed here is exploratory.

## 4. Secondary / exploratory sets (not part of the primary family; no multiplicity claim)
* **E2** (90 episodes; same 30 scenarios, D24R/COUPLED): identity (correct attacker id from t = 0, honest-neighbour tightening β = 0, A_j = A_honest; attacker packet unchanged) · oracle (true state at send time and true ego state, DIAGNOSTIC adapter, β = σ = e = 0, same timing/actuator/W) · emulated trusted relative sensor (error ball 0.10 m / 0.15 m/s, 20 Hz, latency 0.05 s + jitter allowance 0.08 s, range 20 m, no dropout, age propagated exactly, fused with the report enclosure only by valid enclosing-set intersection; disjoint balls ⇒ INVALID_ASSUMPTIONS, never a tiny ball). Reuses E1's standard-information episode of the same scenario. Logged: certification (actual vs standard-information counterfactual), authority reserve γ, contradictions.
* **E3** (80 episodes, seeds 14000–14009; D24R and D0R; COUPLED; attacker slot shifted toward its nearest honest neighbour by 0, 1, 2, 3 m → initial clearance to the victim 6, 5, 4, 3 m; frozen values; see DEVIATIONS D2 for why the certificate transition itself is not bracketable): predicted pairwise certificate status and the restricted-model margin M (diagnostic only) versus measured separation/task completion.
* **E4** (96 episodes, seeds 15000–15005; D24R and D24R_NOSQ; A0 and COUPLED; N ∈ {4, 8}; nominal and adverse network = +0.10 s delay, ±0.02 s bounded jitter, 0.10 independent loss, no partition, identical token bucket 2000 B/s, burst 576 B): small-n transfer/limitation check, per-cell n = 6.
* **E5** (24 episodes, seeds 16000–16011; D24R; COUPLED): (a) report falsification beyond the declared envelope (β_p 3.0 m, β_v 1.5 m/s) and (b) total acceleration beyond the declared envelope (ε = 6 m/s², commanded total 9 m/s² > A_any 8.8; realised acceleration logged, no teleportation). The offline premise audit labels these premise violations; they are **not** counterexamples to a conditional statement.
* **E0** (deterministic, already executed): 1,701 grid cases + 200 slew-limited cases (`results/coupled_safety_v1/e0/`).
Total confirmatory PX4 episodes: 480 + 90 + 80 + 96 + 24 = **770**.

## 5. Validity, exclusions, retries (outcome-independent)
Infrastructure retry: an episode whose container/PX4/DDS failed to produce `eval/summary.json` is re-run once with the same seed (attempt retained under `_failed_attempts/`). RTF rule: an episode with mean RTF < 0.95 is re-run once (slow attempt retained under `_rtf_attempts/`); if still < 0.95 the first attempt is used and flagged. Crashes, flips, failed landings, controller timeouts and certificate failures are **outcomes**, never exclusions; GCS exit code ≠ 0 does not exclude an episode (truth outcomes are used). An episode is analysable iff the truth stream covers the whole encounter window; non-analysable episodes stay in the inventory and are handled by (a) complete-case primary analysis and (b) a sensitivity analysis counting them as min_sep_ha = 0. Workers: start with 2 (the pipeline was validated at RTF ≈ 0.99 with 2); never more unless RTF ≥ 0.95 is verified in a pilot.
